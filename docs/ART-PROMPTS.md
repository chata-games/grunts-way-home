# Original game art

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool for the September 2026 rebuild. PNG outputs were converted to WebP for delivery. The character was resized to 700 × 1050 with its alpha channel preserved. No remote image service runs in the game.

A hand-painted adventure storybook world with sophisticated stylized animated-film concept art quality, detailed painterly textures, rich deep teal foliage contrasted with soft warm golden sunbeams and fresh mint light.
